Key Words:
Hybrid Cloud, Dedicated Physical Connection, Bypass Public Internet, Low Latency, Consistent Bandwith.  

What is it:
This service gives you physical direct link from your on-premises servers to the cloud by a secure, stable, fast connection. The information does not travel by the public internet so it gives you data a more secure path.

Use Cases:
Enterprise workloads requiring data transfer, strict regulatory compliance or consistent network performance.
