Key Words:
Instance Types.

What is it:
Those are the different type of instance billing you can get:
* **On-Demand** Means that you pay only for what you use (second/hour), if an instance is turned off or eliminated, you stop paying for it.
* **Spot** Is an unused instance that you can acquire for a very low cost but if AWS need this instance you are notified two minutes before you got out, so is recommended for works that doesn't get affected by suddenly interruptions of service. Up to 90% saving.
* **Reserved** Means that if you can predict your workloads for the next 1-3 years, you could get a discount for long-term compromise. Up to 72% saving.   

Use Cases:
**On-Demand:** Short term or non predictable workloads.
**Spot:** Workloads that tolerate interruptions.
**Reserved:** Stable and predictable workloads.
