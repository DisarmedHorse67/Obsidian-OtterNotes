Key Words:
Data Migration, On-premises.

What is it:
Physical edge-computing and data-migration appliances, that helps with data transfer of terabytes or petabytes of offline data on places where the connection is slow, bat or unavailable.  

Use Cases:
Data migration in difficult or expensive network access.
