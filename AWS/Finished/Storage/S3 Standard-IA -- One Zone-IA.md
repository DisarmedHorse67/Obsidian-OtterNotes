Key Words:
S3 family, low access data-fast retrieval 

What is it:
This S3 service provides a cheaper storage option for data that doesn't get accessed frequently, but when needed it is accesible in a laps of seconds. One Zone means that the S3 bucket is in only one specific AZ, it is a little bit cheaper but if the AZ suffers an attack or is not available at the moment, you will lose access to your data or even the data itself.  

Use Cases:
Once a month accessed data, long storage data but fast retrieval needed. OZ is used for reproducible data.
