Key Words:
Long term storage, cheap.

What is it:
This services provides vaults and archives for information that is only used once a year or more, it's retrieval will need a couple of minutes (1 - 5 min with Expedited) to hours(3 - 5 hours with Glacier Standard), half a day (5 - 12 hours with Bulk) or a day or two( 12 - 48 hours with Deep Archive) depending on what service is.

Use Cases:
Regulatory archiving, long-term backups where immediate access is **not** required.

Extras:
S3 Glacier instant retrival: msec access
Flexible retrieval: minutes
S3 Flexible retrieval bulk: 5-12 hours
S3 Deep Archive: 12 - 48 hours
